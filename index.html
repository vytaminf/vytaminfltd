The firm

Built around a trader’s view of market structure.

VytaminF Ltd was created around a simple premise: durable trading performance comes from understanding the mechanisms that move risk through a market.

Rather than relying on generic technical signals, the firm focuses on how liquidity is supplied and withdrawn, how participants interact around value, how aggressive flow is absorbed or rejected, and how execution conditions change across regimes.

The business is currently operated by its founder as a one-person proprietary trading and research company. The long-term objective is to compound research quality, execution discipline and infrastructure into an institutionally credible investment operation.

Research agenda

Microstructure first.

Research is organised around the behaviour underneath price: liquidity, intent, positioning, execution and the transfer of risk.

01

Order Flow

Aggressive buying and selling, absorption, trapped positioning, imbalance, initiative activity and the interaction between executed flow and resting liquidity.

FootprintImbalanceAbsorption
02

Market Microstructure

Auction dynamics, liquidity formation, price discovery, queue behaviour, spread, depth, volatility and the conditions that shape short-horizon market behaviour.

03

Volume & Auction Structure

Value areas, volume profiles, failed auctions, acceptance, rejection and the relationship between prior structure and current participation.

04

Derivatives & Volatility

Options-implied information, volatility regimes, positioning effects and the ways derivatives markets can alter intraday behaviour and risk.

05

Execution Research

Entry quality, adverse excursion, path dependency, slippage assumptions, timing, trade management and the difference between a signal and an executable edge.

06

Risk & Robustness

Drawdown behaviour, survival probability, parameter stability, out-of-sample validation and risk rules designed to keep strategy failure measurable.

Research process

From observation to executable evidence.

01

Observe

Identify recurring behaviour in auction structure, order flow or execution.

02

Formalise

Translate discretionary observations into explicit, testable market conditions.

03

Test

Use historical data to measure expectancy, failure modes and regime dependence.

04

Stress

Evaluate robustness, drawdown, parameter sensitivity and out-of-sample behaviour.

05

Execute

Trade only when the research framework and defined risk conditions align.

06

Review

Feed live execution and performance data back into the research process.

Long-term mandate

Build the research foundation before building the institution.

VytaminF Ltd is not presented as a hedge fund today.

The objective is to earn that level of institutional credibility over time through stronger research, deeper market understanding, disciplined risk management, repeatable execution and a documented performance process.

The company's direction is toward a more sophisticated proprietary trading and investment research operation, with any future external capital activity subject to the appropriate legal, regulatory and operational framework.
