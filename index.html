About

A focused trading business built around process.

VytaminF Ltd operates as an independent, founder-led proprietary trading business. The company does not manage external client capital. Its work centers on trading performance in simulated evaluation and funded-account environments offered by proprietary trading firms.

The company combines market research, backtesting, execution review, data analysis, and strict risk controls to develop repeatable trading processes.

Approach

How VytaminF Ltd operates

01

Research

Historical market data is used to test ideas, identify repeatable behaviour, and separate robust setups from noise.

02

Position sizing, drawdown limits, account rules, and failure probabilities are treated as core parts of the strategy rather than afterthoughts.

03

Execution

Trading is rule-driven, with emphasis on discipline, review, and consistency across simulated prop-firm evaluation and funded-account environments.

Research

Data-led market research.

VytaminF Ltd studies futures and derivatives markets using quantitative and discretionary frameworks. Research may include market structure, auction theory, volume profile, order flow, volatility, execution behaviour, and strategy robustness.
