// THE OPERATOR

Built by a trader.
Built to become a firm.

VytaminF Ltd is a one-person proprietary trading and market research company focused on developing an institutional-grade understanding of short-horizon market behaviour.

The company is built around order flow, auction theory, volume profile, execution, derivatives positioning and quantitative validation — not generic technical signals.

Today, the firm operates with proprietary decision-making in simulated prop-firm environments. The long-term ambition is to build the research, process, infrastructure and track record required for a serious investment operation.

The objective is not to look like a hedge fund before becoming one. The objective is to build the research discipline that makes becoming one credible.

// OPERATING PHILOSOPHY

Four rules.

RULE_01

Structure before opinion.

A thesis matters only when the market structure provides a reason for it to exist.

RULE_02

Risk is defined first.

Position size, invalidation and daily loss boundaries are decided before execution.

RULE_03

Evidence over confidence.

Every setup should survive data, regime changes and the possibility that the original idea is wrong.

RULE_04

Execution is the strategy.

A signal without disciplined execution, cost awareness and behavioural control is not an edge.
